# Changelog

## v0.1.62 (2026-11-07)

New:
- **Race reports (Replay Archive):** every race is kept in the `replays` folder with a report per event; press **F1** in the menus to open them, **D** after a race posts the report in #csup-race-results
- **AI Driver Sets:** your own AI names, colours and numbers in three sets, pick one per race (Garage → AI Drivers)
- **AI Weather Sense** (on by default): in Changing weather the AI reads the forecast before it pits
- **Limitless:** Free Play races up to 1000 laps, 1000 minutes or 1000 km
- **Tyre Letters:** every tyre icon shows its compound, S M H I W
- **Time of day:** how fast a changing day runs and when the race starts
- **Tyre compounds:** soft and hard grip and wear are set in the Free Play menu
- **Smoother weather:** rain effects follow your graphics quality
- The Weather mod is now called **More Weather and Tyres**; main switches on the Mods page end in "Mod"
- The setup page row shows **LB / RB** with a controller and **PgUp / PgDn** without one
- **Settings → Mods** is hidden for now; it comes back with the mod manager

Fixed:
- AI qualifies on softs, and soft, medium and hard really differ in grip and wear
- Fair qualifying with Changing weather and Fuel load
- PlayStation buttons on the mod control rows; one button no longer does two things
- The Next tyres pop-up is readable again, smaller, under the weather bar
- Doubled rows in the Free Play setup after turning off the controller

## v0.1.61 (2026-11-02)

New:
- **Start tyres:** with Weather on, a "Start tyres" row in the race setup (Auto / Soft / Medium / Hard) and on the car screen (press **T**: Auto / Soft / Medium / Hard / Inter / Wet). Auto is the AI's own pick
- **Starting fuel countdown:** 5 seconds on the grid with "Press F to change starting fuel", then the tank is locked. Fuel messages now also show with Weather off
- **Controller buttons** for Change tyres, Change fuel amount (new row) and Power boost in Settings → Controls
- **LB / RB** (Page Up / Page Down) switch the Free Play setup between the Game and Mods pages
- **Ghost spectator camera:** turn round the followed car with Q / E, the right stick or the right mouse button; Z / X zoom, C resets
- **Weather strip:** a quarter smaller, hidden while the results table is shown, and the yellow bar is labelled "dry line"
- **Welcome panel:** easier to read; Enter, Esc, Space or A / B close it, D or Y hides it until the next version

Fixed:
- Racing or spectating your own lap as a custom ghost (add yourself with F8)
- The tyre indicator in the standings no longer covers the team colour bar

Under the hood:
- Big Grid: groundwork for larger online grids; nothing changes in single player
